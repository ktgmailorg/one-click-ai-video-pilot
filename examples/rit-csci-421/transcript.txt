Use Concurrency Control and Isolation to diagnose a failure by separating symptoms,

causes, and corrective action.

Isolation controls how concurrent transactions observe one another.

Consider this worked case: Read committed isolation allows multiple transactions to read

data simultaneously but prevents one transaction from seeing uncommitted changes

from another.

Locate the first point where observed behavior diverges from expected behavior, then test the

smallest plausible cause.

The diagnosis is strongest when another person can reproduce the observation and the test.
